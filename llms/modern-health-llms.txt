# Modern Health

> Global mental health benefits platform employers offer to their workforce. The
> Adaptive Care Model spans self-guided digital tools, 1:1 coaching, therapy and
> counseling, group support Circles, and crisis care, matched to members through a
> Stepped Care methodology. Care is delivered in 80+ languages across a proprietary
> provider network spanning 200+ countries and territories. Modern Health does not
> publish a public developer API.
